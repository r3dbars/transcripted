# Sounds

`dictation-start.caf` plays when a dictation starts. It is made from "Button 5" by
skyscraper_seven on Pixabay.

`dictation-stop.caf` plays as soon as you press Stop, before transcription or paste.
It is made from "Button 14" by skyscraper_seven on Pixabay.

Both clips were changed for Transcripted: trimmed to the audible click (0.55s and
0.68s, down from 3.6s and 3.0s), given a short fade-out, level-matched to the other
app cues, and re-encoded as AAC in CAF. They are used under the Pixabay Content
License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).

`dictation-cancelled.wav` and `meeting-transcript-complete.mp3` are the older cues
for cancel/no-speech and a finished meeting transcript.
